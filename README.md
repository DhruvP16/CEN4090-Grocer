# Grocer

Grocer is an app idea that brings grocery prices, weekly ads, coupons, and deals from different stores into one place. Its goal is to help people save time and money when grocery shopping.

## Problem

Grocery prices and deals vary between stores. Comparing them often requires checking multiple apps or websites. The store with the lowest prices may also cost more to visit when considering distance and gas.

## Planned Features

- Search for products and compare prices across nearby stores.
- Create and save grocery lists.
- Compare the total cost of a grocery list at different stores.
- Include coupons, deals, and estimated fuel costs in comparisons.
- Recommend shopping at one store or multiple stores.
- Allow users to share prices, report deals, and write reviews.
- Support dietary preferences, price alerts, and shopping budgets.
